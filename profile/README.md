# WakeUp Labs

We make it safe to automate what moves value.

We design, build, and operate production infrastructure for programmable value: payments, treasury, settlement, and protocol operations. The frame is the same every time: a mandate, a policy, an action, and evidence. We stay after go-live.

## Open source

Public code from systems we have built and operated. Production deployments live on the site. Newer work is under [wakeuplabs-io](https://github.com/wakeuplabs-io). Earlier public repos are on [wakeuplabs](https://github.com/wakeuplabs).

### Arbitrum and Stylus

- [noir-stylus-verifier](https://github.com/wakeuplabs-io/noir-stylus-verifier): UltraHonk verifier compatible with Arbitrum Stylus.
- [assembly-script-stylus-sdk](https://github.com/wakeuplabs-io/assembly-script-stylus-sdk): Stylus contracts in AssemblyScript, for TypeScript developers. Alpha, maintained by WakeUp Labs.
- [arbitrum-connect](https://github.com/wakeuplabs-io/arbitrum-connect): Withdraw from a child chain to its parent, with a fallback when the sequencer is down.

Stylus Mini-Apps, shipped on Arbitrum and hosted in Lemon:

- [BananitaSwap](https://github.com/wakeuplabs-io/Arbitrum-Miniapp1-BananitaSwap): USDC and token swaps through Camelot V3 and Uniswap V3. Live on Arbitrum One.
- [Verifiable randomness](https://github.com/wakeuplabs/ArbitrumMiniApp-2-verifiable-randomness): Asynchronous randomness on Stylus with Chainlink VRF v2.5, locked liability, and a permissionless refund. Reference implementation. Live on Arbitrum Sepolia.
- [Vaulty](https://github.com/wakeuplabs/ArbitrumMiniApp-3-Vaulty): USDC yield aggregator. One deposit splits across Aave, Morpho, Fluid, and Euler. Live on Arbitrum One.
- [Final report](https://github.com/wakeuplabs/ArbitrumMiniApp-4-FinalMilestone): What shipped across the three Mini-Apps, and what we would tell the Stylus team next.

### Optimism and the Superchain

- [optimism-making-impact](https://github.com/wakeuplabs-io/optimism-making-impact): Explore Optimism governance and RetroPGF. Live at [retroimpactguidelines.xyz](https://retroimpactguidelines.xyz).
- [superchain-accounts](https://github.com/wakeuplabs-io/superchain-accounts): Smart accounts for participation across Optimism chains. Live at [superchainaccounts.xyz](https://superchainaccounts.xyz).
- [op-ruaas](https://github.com/wakeuplabs-io/op-ruaas): Rollup as a service on the OP Stack: CLI, marketplace, and console.
- [opid](https://github.com/wakeuplabs-io/opid): Privado ID adapted to run on Optimism (`did:opid`).
- [rfg1-optimism](https://github.com/wakeuplabs/rfg1-optimism): Query any public view function on Optimism at a specific moment in the past. MIT.
- [Optimism workshop, class 2](https://github.com/wakeuplabs/Optimism-Workshop-Clase2): Workshop with OP en Español and L2 en Español. Certificate minter: [nftminterscript](https://github.com/wakeuplabs/nftminterscript).

### Payments and incentives

- [cdp-escrow](https://github.com/wakeuplabs-io/cdp-escrow): Escrow on Coinbase CDP.
- [cdp-collector](https://github.com/wakeuplabs-io/cdp-collector): Collect and distribute funds with Coinbase CDP.
- [velora-distribution-verifier](https://github.com/wakeuplabs-io/velora-distribution-verifier): Check the Merkle roots behind Velora (ParaSwap) reward and refund distributions.

### Bitcoin and privacy research

- [alpen-multisig](https://github.com/wakeuplabs-io/alpen-multisig): Desktop multisig for Alpen and Strata governance. Signers prepare payloads, collect signatures off-chain, and broadcast Bitcoin transactions.
- [starkware-private-erc20](https://github.com/wakeuplabs-io/starkware-private-erc20): Private ERC-20 proof of concept on Starknet. Noir circuits and Cairo contracts. Amounts and counterparties stay hidden.

### Tokens and field systems

- [delegable-token](https://github.com/wakeuplabs/delegable-token): ERC-721 with a delegated user (ERC-4907). Published as `@wakeuplabs/delegable-token`.
- [SiCFoBA](https://github.com/wakeuplabs/chaco-forestal): Forest certification with blockchain. App: [chaco-forestal-client](https://github.com/wakeuplabs/chaco-forestal-client).
